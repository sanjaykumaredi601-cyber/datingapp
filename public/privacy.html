Privacy Policy & Data Protection

Updated: October 2026 • Operator: NextGen IT Global • Domain: findaura.co • Compliance: India DPDP Act 2023 • GDPR • PIPEDA

1. Overview & Data Fiduciary Disclosures

Aura Dating is operated by NextGen IT Global acting as the primary Data Fiduciary. This Privacy Policy details how personal data is collected, processed, and safeguarded across iOS, Android, and findaura.co.

2. India DPDP Act 2023 & Statutory Privacy Protections

Your Rights Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act:
  • Right to Erasure: Purge your profile, biometrics, and chat records within the app or at /delete-account.
  • Right to Data Portability: Export your full personal data payload, mutual consent logs, and audit trails in structured JSON.
  • 48-Hour Location Wipe: Self-service wiping of all 500m grid locations and path logs directly inside Settings.
  • Consent Withdrawal: Revoke consent for proximity radar, marketing, or biometric verification anytime.

3. Live Proximity Radar & Location Privacy

Aura connects singles through mutual proximity while guaranteeing privacy:

  • Foreground-Only Location: Coordinates are queried only when the app is active (NSLocationWhenInUseUsageDescription & ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION). Continuous background tracking is completely disabled.
  • Fuzzy Relative Distance: Exact latitude and longitude are never shown to other users. Only relative distance (e.g. "2.4 km away") and radar angles are rendered.
  • Path-Crossing Echoes: Uses an obfuscated 500-meter geo-grid that automatically expires and purges after 48 hours.

4. 3D Biometric Active Liveness & Photo Verification

  • Active Liveness Detection: We use on-device ML Kit Face Mesh detection to verify Euler head turn angles (yaw/pitch). Biometric vectors are evaluated temporarily to verify human authenticity and prevent catfishing.
  • Photo Storage: Profile photos are securely hosted in Firebase Storage with authenticated read access and access token governance.

5. Voice Intros, Audio & Video Calls

  • Microphone Usage: Microphone access is requested solely to record 30-second audio profile intros and voice messages.
  • Encrypted Calling: Real-time WebRTC audio/video calls require active two-way consent and are protected in our Secret Vault module.
